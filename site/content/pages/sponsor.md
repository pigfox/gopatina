# Sponsor GoPatina

GoPatina is an open-source experiment by [Pigfox](https://pigfox.com) to bring Rust's performance and safety to Go developers. Sponsorship details are being prepared and will appear on this page.

Until then, the most useful support is to try GoPatina on a small program ([get started](/get-started)), and to report what works and what does not on [GitHub](https://github.com/pigfox/gopatina/issues).
